Gamgee is inviting owners of dogs with cancer in Eastern Australia to apply for our personalised mRNA cancer vaccine trial.

Apply for the trial

Why we are running this trial

From Rosie’s story to a future for more dogs.

It began with Rosie. Her experience with a personalised neoantigen vaccine encouraged Paul to explore how this approach could one day be made available to every dogs with cancer.

Before an approach like this can become broadly available, we must study it carefully and support it by evidence for review by the relevant veterinary regulators. This clinical trial is an important step in that process, helping us learn from participating dogs and understand the vaccine more fully.

Who should apply

If your dog has cancer and is currently in Eastern Australia, you’re welcome to apply. Our team will check your eligibility in this trial.

What happens next

You apply. We take it from there.

You do not need to work out whether your dog is eligible before applying. Tell us what you know and our team will review it.

01

Tell us about your dog.

Share the basic details of your dog's diagnosis, where you live and how we can contact you.

02

Our team reviews the application.

Every application is considered individually. We may ask for more information or veterinary records.

03

We contact you directly.

If there may be a suitable next step, we will explain it clearly and answer your questions before anything proceeds.

No public checklist. No guessing.

Different cancers and different dogs can require different considerations. Start with the application and let our team check eligibility with you.
