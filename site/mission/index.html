We are starting with the first personalised mRNA cancer vaccines for dogs, built from each dog's own tumour.

N of 1 medicine is the future.

We can read biology like never before. We can sequence a tumour and see the exact mutations driving it. So why do dogs with cancer still get the same few options?

Same diagnosis. Different biology. The treatment should know the difference. That means moving from treatments designed for populations to a treatment designed for one patient.

The idea is not new. Doing it at scale is. AI is what changed that: one pipeline reads the tumour, finds the mutations that matter, and designs the vaccine to match.

Where we're starting

Why dogs first.

We started with dog cancer because of Rosie. But it is also one of the hardest problems in medicine to solve.

Dogs get the same cancers we get, arising on their own inside working immune systems. Their disease moves fast, so the science answers in years instead of decades. And they are handed the bluntest treatments in medicine.
