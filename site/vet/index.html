Our approach

Every tumor is different.
We start there.

Each design begins with a dog’s own tumor and a matched healthy sample. Comparing their DNA helps identify cancer-specific changes that can inform a personalized mRNA vaccine.

  1. 01

    Understand the tumor.

    A tumor biopsy and matched blood sample provide DNA for sequencing.

  2. 02

    Find candidate targets.

    Use computational analysis to prioritize tumor-specific mutations.

  3. 03

    Design the vaccine.

    Selected targets are encoded into a personalized mRNA vaccine.

  4. 04

    Study the response.

    Clinical follow-up assesses tumor response, quality of life, and safety.
