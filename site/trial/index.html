Study now recruiting · Eastern Australia

Apply for our dog cancer study.

We are recruiting dogs with mast cell cancer in eastern Australia for an investigational study of personalised mRNA cancer vaccines. Submit a short application and our team will contact you to help with further screening.

Apply for the study See how the study works ↓

This is a research study. Applying does not commit you to participate or guarantee enrolment. Your dog’s usual veterinary care remains central throughout.

About the study

What this study is looking at.

The study will investigate a personalised mRNA vaccine approach for dogs with mast cell cancer. Tell us about your dog and our team will help determine whether they may be eligible for screening.

Who can apply

Dogs diagnosed with mast cell cancer who are based in, or able to travel within, eastern Australia.

Every case is reviewed individually

The study approach

A therapeutic mRNA vaccine designed from each dog’s own tumour and blood samples, intended to work alongside—not replace—existing care.

Personalised to each dog

What screening involves

We help coordinate records, screening, sample logistics and updates, working with the study team and your treating veterinarian.

A named team stays in touch

From one dog to a wider study

This study is the next step after Rosie.

Rosie received a personalised mRNA vaccine as part of a broader treatment plan after being diagnosed with mast cell cancer. Her experience was one case—not proof of a cure. We are now recruiting for a study to learn how this personalised approach may work for more dogs.

Study application and screening

How to apply for the study.

You don’t need to have every record ready before you apply. Start with what you know and we’ll help with the rest.

01

Apply for the study

Complete a short application with basic details about your dog, their mast cell cancer diagnosis and where you live.

02

We get in touch

A Gamgee coordinator will contact you, answer practical questions and—with your permission—help gather relevant records from your vet.

03

The study team confirms eligibility

The authorised study team and treating veterinarian assess potential eligibility. If your dog may proceed, you’ll receive the approved study information and consent discussion before deciding.

This is investigational. Participation may or may not help an individual dog. Please do not delay established care while an application is being considered.
